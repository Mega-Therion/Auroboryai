\section{The encoding: SIMD balanced ternary}
\label{sec:encoding}

The loadbearing contribution of the superseded record is the
\key{2-bit packed SIMD ternary encoding}: balanced ternary states
$t \in \{-1, 0, +1\} \equiv \{\mathrm{Abort}, \mathrm{Suspend},
\mathrm{Execute}\}$ bit-packed two bits per trit into 64-bit words,
32 trits per word:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{BitTrit}(t) = \left\{\begin{array}{ll}
00_2 & t = 0\ \mathrm{(Suspend)}\\
01_2 & t = +1\ \mathrm{(Execute)}\\
10_2 & t = -1\ \mathrm{(Abort)}
\end{array}\right.
\qquad
w_{\mathrm{packed}} = \bigoplus_{i=0}^{31}
\mathrm{BitTrit}(t_i) \ll (2i).
\end{equation}
Bitwise $\min$ / $\max$ over packed words implement TRIT\_AND /
TRIT\_OR across all 32 lanes in parallel --- a real, implementable
hardware encoding, kept as the specification core of this folio
\tiertag{empirical} as a design artifact (specified, not benchmarked
in-record).

\section{Definitions, not theorems}
\label{sec:definitions}

The superseded record's ``Theorem~1 (Soft-Gate Relaxation Theorem)''
and ``Theorem~2 (Log-Entropy Impurity Quantum)'' contain no proof
content: each states a formula and then restates it. Under the
isomorphism rule's honesty corollary --- a definition wearing a
theorem's clothes is a definition --- they are relabeled:

\begin{description}
\item[Definition (Soft-gate threshold).] For model entropy
$H \in [0,1]$ and base threshold $\tau_{\mathrm{base}} \in
[0.50, 0.95]$, the effective decision threshold is
\begin{equation}
\tau_{\mathrm{eff}} = \mathrm{clamp}\bigl(0.50,\, 0.95,\,
\tau_{\mathrm{base}}\cdot(1 - 0.2\cdot H)\bigr).
\end{equation}
\item[Definition (Ternary verdict).] For coherence scores $S(P)$,
$S(D(P))$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Verdict}(P) = \left\{\begin{array}{ll}
+1\ \mathrm{(EXECUTE)} & S(P) \ge \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\\
0\ \mathrm{(SUSPEND)} & S(P) < \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\ \mathrm{and}\
S(D(P)) < \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\\
-1\ \mathrm{(ABORT)} & S(D(P)) \ge \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}.
\end{array}\right.
\end{equation}
\item[Definition (Log-entropy impurity quantum).] Each soft-gate
relaxation accrues $\Delta I = 0.15\cdot H$ bits of impurity,
eroding recall weights in subsequent steps.
\end{description}
These are \key{stipulated mechanisms} \tiertag{conjecture}: their
constants (0.2, 0.15, the clamp bounds) are modeling choices, and the
claim that they ``prevent impurity cascade'' is asserted, not shown.

\section{The liveness correction}
\label{sec:liveness}

The superseded record claimed its decision rule ``guarantees
zero-deadlock execution.'' Its own verdict function defines a
\textbf{SUSPEND} branch --- a state in which neither EXECUTE nor
ABORT fires. A system that can SUSPEND repeatedly has not been shown
deadlock-free; the theorem contradicts the paper's own branch
structure and is \textbf{withdrawn}.

The honest replacement, an observation rather than a theorem: the
verdict function is \key{total} --- for every input pair it returns
exactly one of $\{+1, 0, -1\}$ --- so the decider never
\emph{crashes} for lack of a verdict. Whether repeated SUSPEND
constitutes a \emph{stall} (liveness failure) under the soft-gate
dynamics is \tiertag{conjecture} open: no liveness proof is offered
in this record, and the ``entropy scaling eliminates small-model
deadlock'' sentence is struck as unproved.

\section{What was struck}
\label{sec:honestblock}

The superseded record asserted ``94/94 Rust unit tests passing,''
``100\% clean compilation in Lean~4'' under a stated axiom budget,
and ``real-time 60~FPS 3D rendering'' --- with \textbf{no code, no
proof scripts, and no commits deposited in the record}. These claims
are \key{redacted} from this successor, not downgraded: an
unverifiable test claim is not a claim. What would re-establish them:
deposit of the \texttt{chyren-ternary} test logs, the Lean sources
with their axiom traces, and the portal build --- at which point a
further corrected record can cite them. Until then the honest
statement is that this folio is a specification without a deposited
implementation.
